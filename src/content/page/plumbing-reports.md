# Plumbing Investigation & Reporting

Report Plumbing provides clear, practical plumbing investigation reports for **insurance builders, insurers, strata managers, property managers and commercial clients** across Port Macquarie and the Mid North Coast.

Our reports are based on what we find on site — combining practical plumbing experience with diagnostic testing to help establish the likely cause of a problem and what should happen next.

## Investigation and reporting can include

- Escape of water investigations
- Leak detection and fault finding
- Pressure testing
- Thermal imaging
- Water ingress investigations
- Stormwater and drainage investigations
- Positive smoke testing for drainage and odour investigations
- Odour source investigation
- Hot water system fault diagnosis
- Plumbing defect investigation
- Photographic documentation
- Findings and recommended rectification

## Clear information for the next decision

Whether the report is required for an insurance claim, strata matter, property issue or repair decision, our aim is to provide straightforward information that can be understood and acted upon.

With over **25 years experience**, Report Plumbing understands that a useful report needs to do more than describe the damage — it needs to help establish the likely cause and the appropriate next step.
