# Water Ingress Investigations

Water showing inside a property does not always mean the source is directly above the damage.

Report Plumbing investigates water ingress issues across Port Macquarie and the Mid North Coast to help identify where the water is coming from and what needs to happen next.

We work with **insurers, insurance builders, strata managers, property managers, commercial clients and homeowners**.

## Water ingress investigations can include

- Internal and external plumbing checks
- Roof and downpipe leak investigation
- Stormwater investigation
- Pressure testing
- Positive pressure smoke testing
- Odour and smell source investigation
- Leak detection and fault finding
- Wet-area plumbing investigation
- Drainage checks
- Identification of likely water entry points
- Clear written plumbing reports

## Finding the source, not just the symptom

Water damage can appear a long way from the actual fault.

Our focus is on investigating the likely source, documenting what we find and providing practical recommendations for repair or further investigation.

With over **25 years experience**, Report Plumbing combines practical plumbing knowledge with diagnostic investigation and reporting.
